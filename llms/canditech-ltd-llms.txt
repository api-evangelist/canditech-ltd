# Canditech

> Canditech is a skills-assessment and job-simulation platform for hiring teams. It lets employers evaluate candidates' technical and soft skills through customizable assessments, job simulations, video interviews, and cognitive/personality tests before interviewing — with AI-generated role-specific tests, a 500+ test library, and anti-cheating controls. Canditech is API-first and integrates with 40+ applicant tracking systems (ATS).

## About
- Company: Canditech Ltd.
- Website: https://www.canditech.io/
- Product: AI-powered skills assessment, job simulations, and pre-screening for recruitment.
- Security & compliance: ISO/IEC 27001:2013, GDPR (DPA available), AWS-hosted (EU), AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, annual penetration testing.

## APIs
- Enterprise API (private): available to Enterprise customers and partners on request via support@canditech.io. Exposes three core capabilities — list available assessments, send an assessment to candidates, and review candidate results. No public OpenAPI/Swagger is published.

## Integrations
- ATS integrations (40+): https://www.canditech.io/integrations/ — including Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, JazzHR, Jobvite, Teamtailor, BambooHR, HiBob, Recruitee, iCIMS, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Comeet, Pinpoint.
- Other: WhatsApp, SMS, Google Sheets.
